Privacy

TRAAACE runs on your own computer. Your photos, videos and tags never leave it. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Last updated 2 November 2026.

The app

Everything TRAAACE stores is on your computer, in its own folders. Nothing is sent to us.

What it keeps on your computer

  • Projects: your folders, tags, counts, comments, species keys and settings, in a database file.
  • Thumbnails of your photos and videos, so the grid opens quickly.
  • AI suggestions for project photos, and the AI add-on if you install it.
  • A log file to help fix problems. It can include file and folder names. It stays on your computer unless you choose to send it to us.
  • Quick Classify keeps nothing once you close the session, apart from your settings.

When it goes online

  • Only when you set up, update or check for the AI add-on. It downloads a signed list from traaace.ing, the files from GitHub, and, for the experimental NVIDIA add-on, NVIDIA's files from NVIDIA.
  • Those services see your IP address and the files requested, as with any download. Nothing about your photos or tags is sent.
  • Links you click, like the credits, open in your web browser.
  • It doesn't check for updates in the background, and it sends no usage data or crash reports.

This website

What it uses

  • No cookies, analytics or ads, and no outside fonts or trackers.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark) is saved in your browser.
  • To keep the download buttons up to date, your browser asks GitHub for the latest release and keeps a copy until you close the tab.

Hosting and downloads

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the installers are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • NVIDIA's own privacy policy applies to the files the NVIDIA add-on downloads from them.

If you email us

How we use it

  • We use your email, and anything you send like screenshots, test results or logs, only to reply and to improve TRAAACE.
  • We don't sell or share it, and we keep it only as long as it's useful.
  • We may describe what testers report (for example, how fast scans were on a graphics card), but never with your name or email unless you agree.

Your rights

  • Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you: email hello@traaace.ing.
  • If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

If TRAAACE ever starts collecting anything new, this page will say so before it happens. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also the terms and licences.
