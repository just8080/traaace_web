AI models (in the AI add-on)

  • Alita 3.03, the New Zealand species classifier, by Olly and wekaResearch with the Department of Conservation Predator Science team. GPL-3.0. Hosted with Olly's permission. Alita on GitHub.
  • MegaDetector v5a, the animal detector, by Dan Morris and contributors. Its code is MIT-licensed; the v5 model is built on YOLOv5 by Ultralytics, so the add-on's models are distributed under GPL-3.0. MegaDetector on GitHub.

AI suggestions

The AI add-on suggests species and counts. It is a helper, not an expert.

Please keep in mind

  • Suggestions can be wrong, and the confidence shown is a rough guide, not a probability.
  • Check suggestions yourself before you rely on them, especially for pest control, reporting, research or anything with real consequences.
  • You are responsible for the tags you keep and export.

Experimental versions

  • Experimental builds, like the NVIDIA graphics card version, are for testing. They may not work on your computer, and may change or be withdrawn.
  • Keep your photos and exports backed up when you use them.

Your photos and data

Yours, on your computer

  • Your photos, videos, tags and exports stay yours. TRAAACE never uploads them. See the privacy page.
  • Keep your own backups. Quick Classify keeps nothing once you close it, so export first.

Your responsibilities

  • Make sure you are allowed to use the photos you open, for example when cameras are on someone else's land.
  • Trail cameras sometimes photograph people. Handle those photos with care and in line with privacy law.
